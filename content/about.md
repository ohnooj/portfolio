I am completing my PhD in computer science at the [University of Toronto](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu), advised by [Professor Karan Singh](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~karan/) in the [Dynamic Graphics Project lab](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu). My research focuses on in-situ interaction and direct manipulation for creation, design exploration, and interactive graphics applications.

I have served as a course instructor at the University of Toronto and Georgia Tech, where I enjoy teaching and working with students. I care about story-driven lectures and dynamic visualizations that make complex ideas easier to see. I have also worked as a teaching assistant for over 20 semesters.

I completed my B.S. in Computer Science and M.S. in Computer Science (Computer Graphics) at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/), where I worked with [Professor Greg Turk](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~turk/) on 3D fabrication and [Professor Jarek Rossignac](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/gvu/people/official/jarek.rossignac/) on truss-lattice tetrahedral structures.

Outside of research and teaching, I enjoy serving [my church](https://newhopefellowship.ca), buying specialty coffee, and [Olympic Weightlifting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olympic_weightlifting) (snatch: 90kg, clean & jerk: 111kg).
